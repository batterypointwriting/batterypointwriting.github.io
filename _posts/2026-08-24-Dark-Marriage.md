title: Dark Marriage
date: 2026-08-24

Coffee, cigs, and alcohol,<br>
who married these things?<br>
From Turkish to Japanese oji-sans,<br>
to the neapolitan grandads who live by the moment,<br>
I wonder who united these items together.<br>
An unbreakable band, a waltz that needs no explanation.<br>
A short while ago, these items danced together seamlessly<br>
now it's an aesthetic enjoyed by some, a fading culture.<br>
Packed pubs and bars, aperitivos in the corners and alleys of Milan<br>
to the dive bars of America,<br>
these artificats could be found everywhere.<br>
Each place shrowded by a light blue tint from burning tobacco smoke,<br>
Now a forgotten culture.<br>
Yet I, cling on.<br>
A cigarette makes you slow down, relax and breathe.<br>
They forced us to look around, speak to strangers, who then became friends.<br>
A world I wish would come back, where we don't have capitalist vapes,<br> 
a clear indictator that time is running away.<br>
Slow down, look around.<br>
