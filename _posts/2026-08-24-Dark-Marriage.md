title: Dark Marriage
date: 2026-08-12

Coffee, cigs, and alcohol,
who married these things?
From Turkish to Japanese oji-sans,
to the neapolitan grandads who live by the moment,
I wonder who united these items together.
An unbreakable band, a waltz that needs no explanation.
A short while ago, these items danced together seamlessly
now it's an aesthetic enjoyed by some, a fading culture.
Packed pubs and bars, aperitivos in the corners and alleys of Milan
to dive bars of America,
these artificats could be found everywhere.
Each place shrowded by a light blue tint from burning tobacco smoke,
Now a forgotten culture.
Yet I, cling on.
A cigarette makes you slow down, relax and breathe.
They forced us to look around, speak to strangers, who then became friends.
A world I wish would come back, where we don't have capitalist vapes, 
a clear indictator that time is running away.
Slow down, look around.
