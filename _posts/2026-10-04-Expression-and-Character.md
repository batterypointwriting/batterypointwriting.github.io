I have spent most of my life in the UK and I have long reached the point where my English surpasses
my 'first' language. I like being bilingual but I feel very different depending on what language
I'm speaking. Maybe I get perceived as having a different personality. How someone gets perceived
is down to how they express themselves not necessarily who they internally are? Not being able to
find the right word is not great...

I think I must've declined in my other language or more maybe stopped growing in the other language. 
Having my education, work and relationship experiences set in English has developed my character,
but left my other language behind? Not to mention my handwriting in my other language has gotten so
bad it looks like a child's handwriting because I rarely ever need to write!

I think I should start practising my other language more and gain some new experience using it.
