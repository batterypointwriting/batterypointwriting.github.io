Over the last few days I've been wanting to write something, something that I've pondered over. 
I've thought above love, work, life and generally everything in between. 

Alas, the written word eluded me since. So many thoughts, each one trying to pass into ink and yet 
I come up dry. So I'll write about that --- my current inability to express thoughts on paper. Two weeks
ago, on Tuesday 1st, I buried a friend. Not a close friend, yet not a stranger at all. We went school,
sixth form together, and spoke here and there since. He was a great guy, funny, charismatic, caring,
generally a good dude. Unfortunately, God decided that his time was over, and that he needed him by
his side. That's the hope at least. I want to think that there is a place once we die, otherwise its
a bleak existence. 

We study for twenty years, work for forty, and then spend the rest fixing various diseases and problems. 
There needs to be something. Last two weeks I've been having issues with my front door --- either it 
wont close or it won't open. Back and forth between landlord and myself; two weeks of difficult work 
project, struggling to do what is required of me. 

Weekends, busy doing that or this, never ending evenings, spent doing things I'd forget tomorrow, 
making others feel heard and present. While I just wanna get drunk and sleep on a beach where its warm. 
I've got to juggle my responsibilities between friends, parents and partner, to keep everyone happy 
and content. <br>

I want and need a break, to turn off the cranium, shut off this thing for long enough so that it
feels rested. Maybe I'm complaining when I shouldn't, I've got everything others dream of and more,
yet heavy is my head. How I long to breathe crisp autumn air, to walk under the blanket of an October
breeze. To feel nothing but the mild English sun on my face. To end the days sat near a fire, stiff
whisky in one hand, and a cigar in the other. Even as I describe such a day, sat under LED and blasted
with blue light, the mind relaxes. 

This is year one of forty more, and already it seems too much. I wonder what lies ahead. Much less of
an artistic post, but it does reflect my current state. I'll write soon, maybe from Wales.

Let us trouble not, for all shall pass. To greener pastures and cleaner air.
