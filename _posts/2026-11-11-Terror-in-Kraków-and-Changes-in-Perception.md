title: Terror in Kraków and Changes in Perception
date: 2026-11-11
---
Emily and I are sitting in the town square. We each have a cup of mulled wine and are discussing how finances should be dealt with in both platonic and romantic relationships. In the next instant, we hear a number of loud bangs. A large flock of birds lifts in a bulk motion and moves away from the square. I'm quite confused as to what is going on, but I am not alarmed, as loud noises occur quite frequently in public. I turn to see many frightened faces running out of the square, maybe one-hundred people all moving at the same time. I thought that the war had started. I'd never seen so many people looking this panicked. 

We run down the street, and see lots of smoke coming from a driveway. I decide that we don't want to go that way, and we turn around, and turn left again down a smaller street. We enter a restaurant. Many others enter, who have also been running. The crowd is split between those who are scared and those who are not aware that anything is happening. I order tea and we sit down. 

The next hour is the worst, since we still do not know what is going on. I keep refreshing my phone, but there is no news for at least half-an-hour. A few people are tearing up in the room and are holding hands. The restaurant owner is quite jokey and believes nothing has happened, he paces up and down. My perception changes as such: I am hyper aware of my surroundings, and now care about the distance between us and the main square. I often look out the window, expecting some kind of big event. 

Emily and I are both on the phone to our parents explaining the situation. I think she's handling the situation well, all things considered. I never have been hugely emotional in times of crisis, but later it hits me like a train. I phone my parents and ask whether we should move or stay in the same place. After another stressful wait, we receive information that there has been a public shooting and the police has dealt with the criminal. I talk to an older polish couple sitting beside us, and they offer to give us a lift back to our hotel. We leave the restaurant and quickly walk to their car, and they drive us back. We are in a state of shock for the next few hours. 

*Aside:* Kraków has always been a peaceful city to visit. I have shown a few of my friends around, I usually go there to rest, have a few drinks and visit a hipster café in the morning. I hope I can go back without feeling weird about it all.
