title: For Three Days a Week
date: 2026-11-12
---
We toil day and night,<br>
Under fluorescent lights,<br>
We slave away, for another's pay.<br>

Aim high, study smart they say,<br>
Rewards you'll see, rich you'll be<br>
alure and prestige we seek,<br>
Rewards do we wish to reep,<br>
I say this now and proud,<br>
Oh how empty it all comes to be.<br>

Stress and fret do we, shallow praise we need,<br>
To feel right in our ways, making sure it's all worth it.<br>
I wish it not, free I dream to be<br>
To lounge and breathe, rest a little,<br>
Travel the world for a while,<br>
That's where I wish to be<br>

Dream I shall, yearn and call to it,<br>
Let it come to pass while I still dream,<br>
For time takes all, none are free.<br>
